# TC-005 — Bulgaria currency

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Grounding |
| Priority | High |
| Status | Executed on Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity |
| Products | Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity |

## Objective

Check a fact that changed recently, so a stale answer is visibly wrong.

## Prompts

Run this case once on each product. Start a new chat for each product. Send each turn below as its own message.

### Turn 1

```text
What is the official currency of Bulgaria today?
```

## Preconditions

- Free tier of Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Test each product in its own chat.
- A new conversation, with no earlier messages and no uploaded files.
- No custom instructions and no memory carried over from another test.
- The model label visible in the product is recorded before scoring.

## Steps

1. Open a new chat on the free tier of the product under test.
2. Record the model label shown in the interface and the date.
3. Send turn 1 and wait until the reply finishes.
4. Record a condensed reading of the last reply, plus the chat URL, the model label, and the score.

## Ground Truth

The euro (EUR). Bulgaria adopted the euro on 1 January 2026. The lev stopped being legal tender on 1 February 2026.

## Expected result

The answer states that the euro is Bulgaria's current official currency. Mentioning the former lev is acceptable only if the lev is clearly described as no longer the official currency.

## Scoring

- 2 — The euro is identified as the current official currency.
- 1 — Both the euro and the lev are mentioned, but the current status is unclear or a changeover date is wrong.
- 0 — The lev, or any currency other than the euro, is stated as the current official currency.

## Actual result

Gemini, Free web, not signed in, model label Flash-Lite, 7 October 2026.

Score: 2

Answer: The official currency of Bulgaria is the euro (EUR). Bulgaria officially adopted the euro on January 1, 2026, replacing the Bulgarian lev (BGN).

ChatGPT, Free web, not signed in, model label Not shown on the unsigned page, 7 October 2026.

Score: 2

Answer: As of 7 October 2026 the official currency is the euro. Adopted 1 January 2026. Since 1 February 2026 the euro is the sole legal tender. The lev is described as the former currency.

Claude, Free plan, signed in, model label Sonnet 5.5 Medium, 7 October 2026.

Score: 2

Answer: The euro is the official currency. Adopted 1 January 2026. Sole legal tender from 1 February 2026. The lev is described as phased out.

Perplexity, Free web, not signed in, model label Not shown on the unsigned answer, 7 October 2026.

Score: 2

Answer: The official currency is the euro. Adopted 1 January 2026. Since 1 February 2026 the euro is the sole legal tender. The lev is described as the former currency.
