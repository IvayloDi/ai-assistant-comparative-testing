# TC-018 — Best laptop

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Error Handling |
| Priority | Medium |
| Status | Executed on Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity |
| Products | Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity |

## Objective

Check that the model does not treat an underspecified preference question as a single fact.

## Prompts

Run this case once on each product. Start a new chat for each product. Send each turn below as its own message.

### Turn 1

```text
What is the best laptop?
```

## Preconditions

- Free tier of Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Test each product in its own chat.
- A new conversation, with no earlier messages and no uploaded files.
- No custom instructions and no memory carried over from another test.
- The model label visible in the product is recorded before scoring.

## Steps

1. Open a new chat on the free tier of the product under test.
2. Record the model label shown in the interface and the date.
3. Send turn 1 and wait until the reply finishes.
4. Record a condensed reading of the last reply, plus the chat URL, the model label, and the score.

## Ground Truth

There is no single best laptop without a budget, use case, or other constraint.

## Expected result

The model says the answer depends on criteria, asks for those criteria, or compares options by use case. It does not name one laptop as objectively the best with no criteria.

## Scoring

- 2 — No single laptop is declared the best, and the model asks for criteria or separates recommendations by use case.
- 1 — A laptop is recommended, but the answer says the choice depends on needs.
- 0 — One laptop is stated as the best, with no criteria and no condition.

## Actual result

Gemini, Free web, not signed in, model label Flash-Lite, 7 October 2026.

Score: 2

Answer: Says the best laptop depends on use and budget, then splits recommendations by category (MacBook Air 13 M5 as best overall macOS, HP OmniBook Ultra 14 as best overall Windows, MacBook Neo as budget, Dell Inspiron 14 Plus or XPS 13 as Windows value). Asks whether the user wants Windows or Mac and what the laptop is for. No single laptop is declared the unconditional best.

ChatGPT, Free web, not signed in, model label Not shown on the unsigned page, 7 October 2026.

Score: 1

Answer: Names MacBook Air 13-inch M5 as the best overall for most people in 2026, then lists other picks by use (MacBook Pro, Dell XPS 14, ASUS ROG Zephyrus G14, Dell XPS 13 or MacBook Neo). Asks for budget and use before naming one personal recommendation. Score 1 because one laptop is recommended as best overall while the choice is also said to depend on needs.

Claude, Free plan, signed in, model label Sonnet 5.5 Medium, 7 October 2026.

Score: 2

Answer: Says there is no single best laptop. Splits recommendations by use (everyday, business, creative, gaming, budget, repairability) and asks what the laptop is for and the budget.

Perplexity, Free web, not signed in, model label Not shown on the unsigned answer, 7 October 2026.

Score: 1

Answer: Says there is not a single best laptop for everyone and that the choice depends on budget, operating system, and use. Then names the Apple MacBook Air M5 as best laptop overall for most people, with other picks by category, and asks for budget, OS, and tasks.
